1893 Macro

Research and advisory on the public services that shape everyday life.

About

1893 Macro is a non-profit social enterprise that conducts applied research on public and social services. Our work spans policy analysis, programme evaluation, and direct support for the professionals who deliver these services.

Our active programmes cover schooling, transit, macro investing, and technology solutions, with healthcare and housing forthcoming.

Research Areas

Schooling

Active

K–12 public school policy, with secondary focus on evidence-based, research-informed teacher support and classroom practice.

Transit

Active

Public transport planning, accessibility, and regional connectivity.

Macro Investing

Active

Macroeconomic research and investment analysis for public-interest capital allocation.

Technology Solutions

Active

Applied technology and digital tools for public-service delivery and organisational efficiency.

Healthcare

Forthcoming

Access, delivery, and workforce sustainability in public healthcare.

Housing

Forthcoming

Affordability, social housing policy, and tenant protections.
